## Welcome to InsightAI 🚀

**InsightAI** is an AI-driven **fraud risk and intelligence platform** designed to help financial institutions detect, prevent, and investigate financial crime with speed, accuracy, and confidence.

Our APIs and intelligence modules enable real-time decisioning, compliance support, and deep risk analysis across the following core areas:

### Fraud Detection & Prevention
Identify and stop fraudulent transactions in real time using behavioral signals, anomaly detection, velocity checks, and dynamic risk scoring.

### Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
Strengthen regulatory compliance with AI-powered transaction monitoring, alert generation, and investigation workflows aligned with AML requirements.

### Device Intelligence
Detect risky devices and automated behavior through device fingerprinting, session analysis, network intelligence, and correlation insights to prevent abuse and account takeovers.

### Document Forgery Detection
Analyse documents for tampering, inconsistencies, and manipulation using layout analysis, text integrity checks, and cross-field validation to ensure document authenticity.

InsightAI helps teams **reduce false positives**, **accelerate investigations**, and **make confident risk decisions** across the entire customer lifecycle - from onboarding to ongoing transaction monitoring.
